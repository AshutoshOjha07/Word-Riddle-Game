# Word-Riddle-Game
Word Riddle Game

A fun and challenging word puzzle game where players solve riddles to guess the correct word. Perfect for improving vocabulary, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

🎮 Features

Engaging riddles with increasing difficulty.

Hints to help players when stuck.

Score tracking to challenge yourself or compete with friends.

Simple and user-friendly interface.

🚀 How to Play

A riddle will be displayed on the screen.

Type or select your answer.

If correct, you move to the next riddle and earn points.

Play until you finish all riddles or beat your high score!
